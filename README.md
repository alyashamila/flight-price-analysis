# 📊 Flight Price Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

This project visualizes flight price data using Power BI with the Flight Price Prediction dataset.

![Dashboard Screenshot]()

---

## 🔍 Key Features

- **Average Price By Airline**
- **Average Price by Airline and Duration (Hours)**
- **Price vs. Days Left of the Flight**
- **Flight by Departure Time and Class**
- **Filter by Departure City, Destination City, Airline, Departure Time, and Stops.**
- **KPIs**: Average Price, Median Price, Average Duration (Hours), Most Popular Airline.

---

## 🔍 Insights

- **Vistara** has the highest average price per ticket.
- **Non-stop flights** tend to have higher prices but shorter durations.
- Flights by **SpiceJet Airlline** showed the widest price range.
- **Night flights** are more expensive compared to the others.
- **Late Night flights** are more cheapcompared to the others.
- The price of the ticket flight peaked on the **second** day before purchasing the flight.

---

## 🗂 Dataset Source

- [Flight Price Prediction Dataset on Kaggle]([https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shubhambathwal/flight-price-prediction])
- Includes fields: Airline, Arrival Time, Class, Days Left of the Flight, Departure Time, Destination City, Duration (Hours), Flight, Price, Source City, Stops
